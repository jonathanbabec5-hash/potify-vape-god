# Vape God marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance owns the public release and approves campaign copy.

**Audience.** Adult arcade players and community event hosts. **Offer.** Give players a short, repeatable game inside the Greenhouse.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Share an actual final-15-seconds gameplay clip with an invitation to a free score challenge. | Give the audience one verifiable reason to try the project. |
| Days 4–10 | Share the reviewed demo or sample through the project page and one relevant creator or community channel. | Finish one free round. |
| Days 11–28 | Publish a build note showing what the trial revealed and one change made from feedback. | 10 distinct completed rounds. |

### Sales test

Pitch a single credit in a free community challenge. Success is one signed fixed-fee event sponsorship and a delivered result report. Wait for tested scoring before quoting reach. Offer a sponsor a single credited tournament slot after a host, rules and verified scoring are in place.

### Measurement and decision

Use `vape-god-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** Editable website-integrated game source is recovered. The server at the inspected website revision explicitly disables game Seed awards. A separate Replit workspace and any subsequent deployment still need parity checks. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
