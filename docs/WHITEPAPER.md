# Vape God
## Whitepaper · public developer edition

**November 25, 2026 · Recovered web client**

### Goal and audience

Give players a short, repeatable game inside the Greenhouse. The immediate audience is adult arcade players and community event hosts.

### Design and implementation

The browser client runs the timed round and refers to a separate reward service. Art and the recovered client can be inspected independently of that service. The local client holds a session identifier and can reach for a reward endpoint at the end of a qualifying round. Run the public preview on its own origin without a reward service.

### What a contributor can establish

The client compiled with React and Vite. In local Chromium, Start Class opened a timed round with the Hold to Hit control. Reward delivery was outside the test. A local score can establish gameplay. An independently verifiable tournament needs authoritative scoring and a way to handle disputed results.

### Evidence and outstanding work

Original editable Replit project and reward server are unavailable. Seeds are non-transferable. The next acceptance case is concrete. Replay a local round with a demo account state and verify that inspecting the client creates no reward claim.

### Place in POTIFY

Offer a sponsor a single credited tournament slot after a host, rules and verified scoring are in place. The marketing plan gives the test window and the evidence for a commercial outcome.
